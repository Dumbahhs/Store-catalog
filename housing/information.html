Own Your Space

Player Housing

Purchase a property on Skyfall Roleplay and make it your own. Housing comes with full interior access, personal storage, and more.

Process

How It Works

1
Browse Available Properties

Check the housing listings in our Discord or store to find a property that suits your character.

2
Open a Discord Ticket

Head to our Discord and open a housing ticket to express your interest and reserve a property.

3
Staff Confirmation

A staff member will confirm the property is available and walk you through any customisation options.

4
Purchase Link Sent

Once confirmed, you'll receive a private Tebex purchase link for the selected property.

5
Move In

After payment is confirmed, the property is assigned to your character. Keys are yours — move in and make it home.

Features

What's Included

Every player-owned property comes with the following as standard:

✓
Full interior access locked to your character
✓
Personal stash / storage inside the property
✓
Ability to invite and manage trusted guests
✓
Unique address and map blip on the server
✓
Property is bound to your character — not transferable by default
Disclaimer

Important Notice

⚠️
Properties are non-transferable. Housing purchases are tied to the purchasing player's character. Staff are not obligated to transfer ownership under any circumstances.
🔒
Banned players forfeit their property. Players permanently banned for rule violations are not entitled to refunds or property transfers. The property will be returned to the available pool.
ℹ️
Supply is limited. There are a finite number of properties on the server. Once all units are sold, no further housing will be available until a property is released.
