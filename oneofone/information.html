1-of-1 Custom Orders

Submit your own asset for a fully unique, server-exclusive implementation. Only one person can ever own each approved 1-of-1 item — there are no duplicates.

Process

How It Works

1
Open a Discord Ticket

Head to our Discord server and open a ticket in the designated 1-of-1 submissions channel.

2
Submit Your Asset

Provide the asset files, a description, and any relevant details about the item you'd like added.

3
Staff Review

Our team will review the submission for technical compatibility, server rules compliance, and copyright concerns.

4
Private Purchase Link

If approved, you'll receive a private Tebex purchase link generated specifically for your order.

5
Implementation

After payment is confirmed, our team begins implementation. You'll be notified when the item is live on the server.

Eligibility

Asset Requirements

Player-submitted assets must meet all of the following criteria before they can be reviewed:

✓
Must follow all server rules and community guidelines
✓
Must meet our technical requirements — correct file format, optimized polygon count, proper LOD setup
✓
Must not violate any copyrights, trademarks, or intellectual property rights
✓
Must comply with Tebex Terms and Conditions for virtual item sales
✓
Submitter must have the legal right to provide the asset for use
✓
Asset must not be a copy of an item already implemented on the server
Disclaimer

Important Notice

⚠️
Approval is not guaranteed. Staff reserve the right to deny any submission at their full discretion, with or without providing a reason. Denial does not entitle the submitter to any compensation.
🔒
By submitting, you confirm ownership. Submission of an asset confirms that the customer has the legal right to provide the asset for use. Throttle RP bears no liability for copyright disputes arising from player-submitted content.
ℹ️
1-of-1 means truly exclusive. Once an item is implemented as a 1-of-1, it will never be added to the general store or sold to another player. Your item remains permanently unique to you on this server.
